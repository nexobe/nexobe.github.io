SchoolPing

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

SchoolPing is a privacy-first school arrival app from Nexobe. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what never leaves your device.

Location stays on your iPhone

SchoolPing uses on-device geofencing only to notice when you arrive at school and to estimate when you should leave. GPS coordinates, routes, and location history stay on the phone. We transmit a boolean arrival event — who arrived, which children, and when — never a map point.

Account data

When you create an account we store your email, name, optional phone number, optional vehicle description, children you add, authorized pickup contacts, daily plans, and arrival events for your school.

What we do not sell

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your location for advertising. We do not share student records with third-party marketers.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. You can delete your account from Settings → Delete Account in the iOS app. Email asghar@nexobe.com if you need help.
