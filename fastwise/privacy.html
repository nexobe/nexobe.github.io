Cutoff Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. Cutoff (formerly Fastwise) does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no backend server, no analytics and no advertising SDK. The procedures, stop times and medication reminders you enter are stored only on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and never name your procedure. Purchases of Cutoff Pro are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Cutoff is not directed at children. If you have questions, email asghar@nexobe.com.
