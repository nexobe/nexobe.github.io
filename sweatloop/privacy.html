Sweatloop Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Sweatloop is an on-device interval timer. It does not collect personal data, does not track you, and does not make network calls for analytics, advertising, or accounts.

Data Not Collected

Apple’s App Privacy label for Sweatloop is Data Not Collected. We do not collect your name, email, workouts, history, location, identifiers, or usage metrics.

What stays on your iPhone

Workouts, an in-progress session, history, and preference toggles are stored locally (UserDefaults and files in the app’s Application Support directory). Deleting the app deletes that data. There is no cloud sync and no account.

Purchases

Sweatloop Pro is a one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Restore Purchases asks StoreKit whether this Apple ID already owns Pro.

Optional notifications

If you turn on phase-change notifications in Settings, iOS may ask for permission at that moment — never at launch. Those notifications are scheduled locally on the device.

Required-reason APIs

Sweatloop uses UserDefaults to remember on-device preferences (reason CA92.1: access information from the app itself). It does not use the data for tracking.
