Help & Support

Email asghar@nexobe.com. We typically reply within one business day. Include your school name and the email on the account.

Auto check-in is off

Open Settings → Location Permissions in the iOS app and set Location to Always. You can still tap I’m Here for a manual check-in.

Join a school

Ask the front office for your 6-character school code, or scan the school’s QR poster during onboarding.

Delete your account

Go to Settings → Delete Account in the iOS app. This permanently removes your SchoolPing login and family data we store.
