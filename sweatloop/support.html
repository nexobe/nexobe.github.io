Sweatloop Support

Sweatloop is a paid-once, on-device interval timer. No account is required.

Resume a workout

If you leave mid-session or the app is killed, reopen Sweatloop. A banner offers to resume the same workout and round. Timing is based on real timestamps, not tick counting.

Cues over music

Keep playing your own audio. Sweatloop uses the playback audio session with mix-and-duck so beeps and voice sit on top, then step aside. Background audio is enabled so cues can continue with the screen locked.

Notifications

Phase-change notifications are off until you enable them in Settings. Sweatloop will not ask at launch.

Sweatloop Pro

Product ID com.nexobe.sweatloop.pro.lifetime is a one-time unlock for unlimited custom workouts, full history, extra themes, CSV export, and compound workouts. Use Restore Purchases in Settings if you reinstall.

Free limits

Built-in templates, Quick Start, three custom workouts, and seven days of history are included.

Contact
