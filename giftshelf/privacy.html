GiftShelf Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026.

GiftShelf does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no backend server, no analytics and no advertising SDK. Your gift ideas windows, optional weight entries and settings are stored only on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Purchases of GiftShelf Pro are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

GiftShelf is not directed at children. If you have questions, email asghar@nexobe.com.
