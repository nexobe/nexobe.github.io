How to play

Drag to steer your puff through the storm. Skim past storm cells, lightning bars and cloud walls for near misses; each one charges your Surge meter. When it is full, tap to hyper-dash and smash through anything in your path. Grab Shield, Magnet and Slow-mo power-ups along the way.
