Puffwing Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026.

Puffwing does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no backend server, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. Your best scores and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Puffwing is not directed at children. If you have questions, email asghar@nexobe.com.
